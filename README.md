## What Is Crypto Mixing?

**Crypto mixing** breaks the public link between the coins you send and the coins you receive. A crypto mixer pools funds from many users and pays out different coins to fresh addresses, so blockchain analysis can't easily follow your transactions.

Each coin works a little differently, so the right approach depends on what you're mixing. Full guides are on **[cryptomixing.net](https://cryptomixing.net/)**.

## Crypto Mixer Cheat Sheet by Coin

| Coin | Traceability | Network fee | Typical mixing time | Key tip |
|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|---|
| **BTC** | High | Medium | 1 – 24 h | Mix when the mempool is quiet |
| **LTC** | High | Low | 30 min – 12 h | Check the pool has enough liquidity |
| **ETH** | Very high | Gas, varies | 30 min – 24 h | Don't fund gas from your old address |
| **BCH** | High | Very low | 30 min – 12 h | Fewer services, so verify links twice |
| **XMR** | Low | Low | — | Private by default, usually no mixing needed |

## Crypto Mixing Safety Checklist

- [ ] Verified official link, opened in Tor Browser
- [ ] PGP-signed letter of guarantee saved
- [ ] Two or more fresh receiving addresses
- [ ] Random delay set
- [ ] Fee in the normal 1–5% range
- [ ] No registration, email or ID requested

## Crypto Mixing FAQ

### Can I mix Ethereum?
Yes, through services or smart-contract pools that support ETH. Be careful with gas: topping up a fresh address from an old one links them again.

### Which coin is the easiest to mix?
Litecoin and Bitcoin Cash are cheap and fast. Bitcoin has the largest pools.

### Is CryptoMixing.net a mixer?
No. It's a guide and review resource and doesn't handle funds.
